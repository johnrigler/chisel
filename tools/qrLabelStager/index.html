QR Label Stager

Separate from QR Field. Uses Avery 5160 geometry: 2.625 × 1 inch labels, 3 × 10 per sheet. Stage only the labels you want, including partially used sheets.

Persistence

In seeded mode each saved label gets its own random suffix. The suffix is generated once when the slot is staged, so reprinting the sheet preserves the same QR values.
