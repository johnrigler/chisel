v1 specimen rules

    
The root payload is 01 || previous-height-u24be || previous-tx-index-u8 || tail-bytes. The predecessor transaction contains the first raw McDougall bytes. This first resolver deliberately implements the two-transaction Litecoin specimen only. It is not yet a general recursive or cross-chain format.
